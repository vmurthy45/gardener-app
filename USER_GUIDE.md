# Rounds user guide

Rounds keeps track of your clients, regular jobs, daily run, quotes and invoices. It's built for gardeners working from a phone, so the buttons are big enough to use with gloves on.

**Before you start:** everything you enter is saved **on your phone only**. There's no account and no cloud copy. See [Back up your data](#back-up-your-data).

The five tabs along the bottom of the screen are **Today**, **Schedule**, **Clients**, **Invoices** and **Settings**. On a computer they appear down the left-hand side.

---

## Install the app on your phone

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open the app's web address in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. From now on, open Rounds from the new icon, **not** from Safari.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the app's web address in Chrome.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

> Install the app *before* you enter any data. On iPhone, Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate data, so anything entered in Safari won't show up in the installed app.

Once it has loaded, the app works without a signal.

---

## Try the app with sample data

You can explore with made-up clients before entering your own.

1. On the **Today** tab, tap **Or try it out with sample data**.
   (Or go to **Settings** → **Your data** → **Load sample data**.)
2. When you're ready to start for real, go to **Settings** → **Erase everything**, then set up your own details.

---

## Set up your business details

Your business details appear on every quote and invoice, so do this first.

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Business**, fill in your business name, your name (used to sign your messages), phone, email and postal address.
3. Under **GST**:
   - Tick **I'm registered for GST** if you are, and enter your **GST number**.
   - Choose whether the prices you enter are **Excl. GST** (the app adds 15%) or **Incl. GST**.
   - If you're not GST registered, untick the box. Your invoices will then say "Invoice" instead of "Tax invoice" and show no GST.
4. Under **Invoices**:
   - Enter your **Bank account name** exactly as your bank has it (e.g. *GREEN THUMB GARDENING LIMITED*). NZ banks check that the name matches the account, so a mismatch can make clients' payments get flagged.
   - Enter your **Bank account number**. You can type just the digits and the app adds the dashes, e.g. *12-3456-0123456-00*.
   - Choose your **payment terms** (e.g. 14 days, or the 20th of the next month).

   Invoices and payment messages show the account name in capitals with the number on the line below:

   ```
   GREEN THUMB GARDENING LIMITED
   12-3456-0123456-00
   ```

Changes save automatically. There's no Save button.

## Add a job

A job is the work you do at an address, such as a fortnightly mow or a monthly hedge trim. Once it's set up, its visits appear on your schedule automatically. You don't need to add the client first.

1. On **Today**, tap **+ Add a job** (under today's run), or **+ Job** at the top right. **+ Job** is also on the **Schedule** tab.
2. Under **Who is it for?**:
   - pick an existing client and address from the list, **or**
   - choose **+ New client or address**, then enter the **street address** (required) and any of: **Client name**, **Mobile**, **Email**, suburb, postcode and **Site notes**.
     If you leave the name blank, the address is used instead and messages start "Hi there". You can add the name later under **Clients**.
3. Under **What's the job?**, tap a service (e.g. **Lawn mow**, **Hedge trim**, **Garden tidy**) or type your own.
4. Enter the **Price per visit**.
5. Under **How often?**, choose **Weekly**, **Fortnightly**, **Every 3 weeks**, **Every 4 weeks**, **Monthly**, **Every 2 months**, **Every 3 months** or **One-off**.
6. Optional: under **In winter**, pick a slower frequency for the winter months (e.g. fortnightly mowing becomes every 4 weeks), or **No visits in winter**.
7. Set the **First visit** date and, if it matters, a **Time**.
8. Optional: set a **Last visit** date if the job has an end date.
9. Optional: add **Notes for this job**, but only if it's more than the standard service (e.g. "Also weed the front bed", "Mow on the high setting"). Leave it blank for a standard mow, trim or tidy.
10. Tap **Save job**.

The app tells you when the first visit is. Visits fill your schedule about nine weeks ahead and keep topping up as time goes on.

You can also add a job from a client's page: open the client and tap **+ Add a job here** under the right property. The client and address are then already filled in.

## Add a client

Use this to add a client before you have a job for them. Otherwise it's quicker to [add a job](#add-a-job) and enter the client there.

1. Go to **Clients** and tap **+ Add** (top right).
2. Enter their **Name**, plus a **Mobile** and/or **Email** so you can message them from the app.
3. Under **Property**, enter the address where you'll be working: street, suburb, city or town, and postcode. Rural addresses with an RD number go in the street line, e.g. *112 Old North Road, RD 2*.
4. Add any **Site notes** (see [Add site notes to a property](#add-site-notes-to-a-property)).
5. Under **How do you bill them?**, choose:
   - **After each visit**: you're offered an invoice every time you finish a job there.
   - **Monthly**: finished visits pile up so you can invoice them all at once.
6. Tap **Save client**.

If you entered an address, the app takes you straight to setting up their regular job (see [Add a job](#add-a-job)).

## Add another property for a client

Use this for clients with more than one site, such as a body corporate or a client with a holiday home.

1. Go to **Clients** and tap the client.
2. Tap **+ Add another property**.
3. Enter the address and site notes, then tap **Save property**.
4. You'll go straight to adding a job for that property.

---

## Add site notes to a property

Site notes are things you need to know on the day: gate codes, dogs, plants not to touch. They appear in orange whenever you open a visit, and the job is flagged **⚠ Notes** on Today's list.

1. Open the client and tap **Edit** next to the property.
2. Tap a quick note (e.g. **Gate code…**, **Dog on site, shut the gate**) or type your own in the box. Put each note on its own line.
3. Tap **Save property**.

---

## Add a one-off job

1. Tap **+ Add a job** on **Today** (or **+ Add a job here** on the client's page) and choose who it's for.
2. Fill in the job and price, and under **How often?** choose **One-off**.
3. Set the date under **First visit** and tap **Save job**.

---

## Change a job's price, frequency or notes

1. Open the client and tap the job (under its property).
   Or, from any visit, tap **Edit this job ›**.
2. Make your changes and tap **Save changes**.

- **Price changes** apply to visits you finish from now on.
- **Frequency or date changes** rebuild the upcoming visits on the new pattern. Visits you've already done aren't changed.

---

## Stop or pause a job

1. Open the job (see above).
2. Tap **End this job (stop future visits)**. Upcoming visits are removed, but past visits and invoices stay.
3. To start it again later, open the job and tap **Restart this job**.

**Delete job** is only available for jobs with no finished visits. For anything with history, use **End this job** so your records stay.

---

## Work through today's run

1. Open the **Today** tab. Your visits for the day are listed in order, with the time, address and job.
2. Tap a visit to open it. From there you can:
   - tap **Maps** to get directions
   - tap **Call** or **Text** to contact the client
   - read the **Site notes** (for the property) and **Job notes** (anything beyond the standard service)
   - type a **Note for this visit** (e.g. "Back lawn too wet, skipped the edges")
3. When you're finished, tap **✓ Mark done** (see [Mark a job as done](#mark-a-job-as-done)).

The top of the Today screen shows how many visits you've done and the value of the work done today.

---

## Change the order of today's visits

1. On **Today**, tap **Change order**.
2. Use the **▲** and **▼** buttons to move visits up or down.
3. Tap **Done**.

---

## Mark a job as done

1. Open the visit and tap **✓ Mark done**.
2. Choose what happens next:
   - **Create invoice now**: makes an invoice for this visit, ready to send.
   - **Invoice later** (or **Back to today** for monthly clients): the visit is added to the **To invoice** list.

Marked done by mistake? Open the visit and tap **Undo: not done yet**. This only works if it hasn't been invoiced yet.

---

## Skip a visit

Use this when a client is away or cancels and the visit won't be rescheduled.

1. Open the visit and tap **Skip this visit**.
2. To bring it back, open it again and tap **Un-skip this visit**.

Skipping one visit doesn't affect the rest of the regular job.

---

## Push today's jobs to another day (rain day)

1. On **Today**, tap **🌧 Rain day: push … to …**.
2. The next working day is chosen for you. Change the date if you need to.
3. Tap **Push … visits**. Every visit not yet done today moves to that day, after the visits already booked there.
4. A list of the affected clients appears. Tap **Text** or **Email** next to each one to open a ready-written message ("rain's stopped play today, so I'll be round on…"), then send it from your phone's Messages or Mail app.

Visits already marked done stay on today.

---

## Catch up on missed visits

If a visit from an earlier day wasn't done or moved, an orange box appears on **Today**.

- Tap **Move all to today** (or **Move it to today**) to bring them into today's run.
- Or tap **Skip** next to any visit you won't be doing.

---

## Move a visit to a different day

**On your phone**
1. Open the visit (from **Today**, **Schedule** or the client's page).
2. Under **Move to another day**, pick the new date and tap **Move**.

**On a computer**
1. Go to **Schedule**.
2. Drag the visit onto another day.

Moving one visit doesn't change the rest of the regular job.

---

## View your schedule

1. Go to **Schedule** to see the current week, Monday to Sunday.
2. Each day shows its visits, how many there are and their total value. Today is outlined in black.
3. Use **‹** and **›** to go back or forward a week, and **Back to this week** to return.
4. Tap any visit to open it.

---

## Send "coming tomorrow" reminders

1. On **Today**, tap the **Tomorrow: … visits** box.
   To remind clients about a different day, change the date under **Visits on**.
2. Tap **Text** or **Email** next to each client. Your phone opens a ready-written message. Check it and tap send.
3. A **✓ sent** tick shows who you've messaged.

To change the wording, see [Change the wording of your messages](#change-the-wording-of-your-messages).

---

## Create an invoice

**For one finished visit:** tap **Create invoice now** after marking it done, or open the visit and tap **Create invoice**.

**For several visits at once (e.g. monthly clients):**
1. Go to **Invoices** → **To invoice**. Clients with finished visits not yet invoiced are listed.
2. Tap **Create invoice** next to the client. Each visit becomes a line on one invoice.
   (Or open the client and tap **Create invoice** in the orange box.)

**From scratch:** go to **Invoices** and tap **+ Invoice**, or open a client and tap **New invoice**.

On the invoice screen you can:
- change the **Date** and **Due** date
- edit any line's description, quantity (the small box) or price
- tap **+ Add item**, or a quick extra like **+ Green waste dump fee** or **+ Mulch**
- tap **✕** to remove a line
- add **Notes** to show on the invoice

GST and the total update as you type. Invoice numbers are filled in automatically.

---

## Send an invoice

1. Open the invoice.
2. Tap one of:
   - **Email to client**: opens your email app with the full invoice details and your bank account in the message.
   - **Text to client**: the same, as a text message.
   - **Print / save PDF**: opens a printable tax invoice. Use your phone's print screen to save it as a PDF, for example to attach to an email yourself.
3. Send the message from your phone.

When you email or text an invoice, it's marked **Sent** automatically. Messages are never sent without you: the app only opens your email or messages app with the text ready to go.

---

## Mark an invoice as paid

- Go to **Invoices** → **Unpaid** and tap **Paid ✓** next to the invoice.
- Or open the invoice and tap **Paid** at the top.

Paid invoices move to the **Paid** tab, which also shows what you've been paid this month and since 1 April (the start of the financial year).

---

## Chase an overdue invoice

1. Go to **Invoices**. The top box shows how much you're owed and how many invoices are overdue.
2. Under **Unpaid**, overdue invoices are listed first, in red.
3. Tap **Remind** next to the invoice, then **Text** or **Email** to open a polite, ready-written reminder that includes your bank details.
4. The invoice then shows when you last sent a reminder.

---

## Write a quote

1. Go to **Invoices** → **Quotes** and tap **+ Quote**.
   Or open a client and tap **New quote**.
2. Choose the **Client** and set **Valid until**.
3. Add the items and prices, just as on an invoice.
4. Tap **Email to client**, **Text to client** or **Print / save PDF**.

---

## Turn an accepted quote into a job or invoice

1. Open the quote and tap **Accepted** at the top.
2. Then:
   - tap **Turn into invoice** to create an invoice with the same items, and/or
   - tap **Set up the job** to schedule the work. The job form opens with the quote's details filled in. Check the date and tap **Save job**.

---

## Edit or delete a client

1. Go to **Clients**, tap the client, then tap **Edit** (top right).
2. Change their details and tap **Save changes**.
3. To remove the client entirely, tap **Delete client and all their data**. This deletes their properties, jobs, visits, quotes and invoices, and can't be undone.

To find a client quickly, use the search box at the top of **Clients**. It searches names, streets and suburbs.

---

## Change the wording of your messages

1. Go to **Settings** → **Message wording**.
2. Edit any of the messages: **"Coming tomorrow" reminder**, **Rain-day message**, **Invoice email / text**, **Quote email / text** and **Overdue reminder**.
3. Words in curly brackets are filled in for you, e.g. `{first}` (client's first name), `{day}` (the visit day), `{amount}`, `{due}`, `{bank}`. Each message lists the ones it can use.

To change the name a client is greeted by (e.g. "Karen" for a body corporate), edit the client and fill in **Name to use in messages**.

---

## Set your working days and winter months

Go to **Settings**:
- **Working days**: tick the days you work. Rain-day pushes go to your next working day.
- **Winter months**: tick the months when jobs switch to their winter frequency. May–August is ticked to start with.

---

## Back up your data

Your data lives only on your phone. If the phone is lost, broken or reset without a backup, the data is gone. The app reminds you on the Today screen if you haven't backed up for two weeks.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Your data**.
2. Tap **Download backup**.
3. Save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or email it to yourself.

---

## Restore from a backup or move to a new phone

1. Install the app on the new phone (see [Install the app on your phone](#install-the-app-on-your-phone)).
2. Go to **Settings** → **Your data** → **Restore from a backup**.
3. Choose your backup file and confirm.

> Restoring **replaces** everything currently in the app with what's in the backup.

---

## Start again from scratch

1. Go to **Settings** → **Your data** → **Erase everything**.
2. Confirm twice.

This deletes every client, job, visit, quote, invoice and setting on this device. Take a backup first if you might want anything back.
